Playthrough Plan
Most daily SC has to be played once before it can be redeemed. What that costs is arithmetic: SC played × the game's house edge. Whether you get there at all is variance. So play the lowest-edge game at its lowest-variance setting, at the smallest bet, and stop when the playthrough clears.
01 Plinko Low risk · most rows · smallest bet. About 1% edge: 1 SC played costs about 0.01 SC. Low risk keeps nearly every drop near 1×, so a small balance survives the whole playthrough.
02 Dice, Limbo, Mines Set the highest win chance the game allows (a 1.01× to 1.05× target) at the smallest bet. Also about 1% edge, and the swings are the smallest of any game: the slow, boring way through is the point.
03 Blackjack Smallest bet, basic strategy, the same bet every hand. About 0.5% edge when played right, several times that when not. Never raise the bet after a loss; a losing run at higher stakes is how a balance dies before the playthrough is done.
04 Baccarat, Roulette Baccarat: banker only, 1.06% edge. Roulette: one even-money outside bet, 2.7% on a single-zero wheel and 5.3% with two zeros, so it is the fallback when nothing above is offered.
05 Slots Smallest bet, every line on, never a bonus buy. 3% to 6% edge, so 1 SC costs 0.03 to 0.06 SC, and the swings are the widest here. Use a slot when the platform offers nothing else, and pick the lowest-volatility one.
06 Worked example A 1 SC daily with a 1× playthrough: through Plinko you expect to keep about 0.99 SC of it; through a slot, 0.94 to 0.97. Over 300 days that gap is several SC of pure edge.

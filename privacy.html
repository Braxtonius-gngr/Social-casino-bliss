Privacy

Last updated 2026-09-27

Daily Roll Over is a tracker for sweepstakes casino daily bonuses. It works without an account, keeps your data on your device first, and sends things off it only for the features below.

Stored on your device

Your tracked platforms, timers, tags, notes, collection history, Daily Run history, ledger and settings are kept in this browser (localStorage and IndexedDB). Clearing site data removes them unless Cloud Sync has a copy.

Cloud Sync (on by default, can be turned off in Settings)

Cloud Sync signs you in with an anonymous account and stores a copy of your tracked platforms and collection history so they survive a cleared browser and can move between devices. If you link an email, it is stored so you can recover the account; we use it only for sign-in and account recovery.

The platform catalogue

The catalogue of platforms and their rules is served to your device's account rather than published as a file. Each day we count how many times each account downloads it and checks it for changes, and how often each platform's link is followed, so that bulk copying stands out. Those counts hold no notes, deck contents or page history.

Usage events (only while Cloud Sync is on)

A short, fixed list of events is recorded against your anonymous account to measure whether the app is working: opening the app (and which link brought you, such as a platform guide page), adding a platform from the catalogue, starting and finishing a Daily Run, turning on background alerts or new-drop alerts, and starting or completing an upgrade. Events never include your notes, emails or deck contents. Turning off Cloud Sync stops them.

Background alerts (off unless you turn them on)

If you turn on alerts “when the app is closed”, your browser’s push address and your upcoming alert times and counts (for example “3 platforms ready at 7:00 PM”) are stored so the server can wake your device. Turning the setting off deletes that record.

If you also turn on alerts for new drops, the names and web addresses of the platforms you track are stored with that record (in a reduced form such as “chumbacasino” and “chumbacasino.com”), so the server can alert only the devices tracking a platform when a drop for it is posted. When one arrives, your device fetches the drop from the public Freebies feed. Turning either setting off removes them.

Community features (only if you use them)

Drops, votes, reports and redemption-speed reports you submit are stored with your account. Published drops and aggregated speed statistics are shown to everyone; who submitted them is visible only to moderators.

Payments

Pro subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We never see your card details. We store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs and your subscription status, and pass your email to Stripe when we have one.

Feedback

Messages sent with the in-app Feedback button, including the technical details it attaches, are delivered to the operator’s private Discord channel.

Page-view statistics

These pages use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: which page was viewed, the referring site, and the browser and country, reported in aggregate. It sets no cookies, does not track you across sites, and is separate from the app’s own usage events above.

Services involved

Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions), Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (hosting and page-view statistics), your browser’s push service (background alerts), jsDelivr (serves the Supabase client library), Google Fonts and Discord (feedback). Links to casino platforms may carry referral codes; what those sites collect is governed by their own policies.

Your choices

Turn off Cloud Sync or background alerts in Settings at any time, and export or delete your local data from Settings.

To delete your cloud data, use Settings → Delete My Cloud Data. It permanently deletes this device’s cloud account and everything stored with it: the synced platforms and collection history, background alert records, usage events, community submissions and any linked email. If you have a Pro subscription, cancel it first; once it has ended, the deletion goes through. Stripe keeps its own payment records for accounting. You can also ask for deletion through the Feedback button.

# Brief for the round's one auditor (paired packet)

You are an independent visual auditor. You have not seen this work before. This is a read-only job: look at the images and write your report; do not edit, create or run anything in the repo.

THE PIECE
<two or three sentences: what the piece is, the owner's visual target, the look each part is meant to carry. Point to ref/ (the owner's target image and the real reference).>

WHAT YOU GET
- ref/: the owner's target and the real reference. The bar is the app's R×R card-table home (ref/sheet-rxr.png), a 7/10.
- pairs/: images P1, P2, ... Each shows the SAME crop twice, LEFT and RIGHT, from two builds of the piece in an order you are not told. One side is the current round, the other the previous round. The crop's cluster name is in the label.
- frames/: the frames this round changed, from the current build only, for context.

ORDER OF WORK
1. Read ref/ first.
2. For each pair, decide which side is closer to the owner's target FOR THAT CLUSTER, or SAME if you cannot tell, and say in a few words what makes the difference. Judge the cluster named in the label; ignore everything else in the crop.
3. Then look at frames/ and write findings as below, tagged with the closed id list.
4. Last, the one line that matters most: is the piece as a whole closer to ref/owner-target.png than ref/sheet-rxr.png is to its own bar, yes or no, and in what one respect it most falls short.

WHAT TO RETURN (exactly this layout; a script reads it)

P1: LEFT|RIGHT|SAME | <why, a few words>
P2: ...
SCORE: <1-10, half points; the rubric scale below>
FINDINGS (at most 8, most severe first):
<ISSUE-ID> | SEV <1-3> | <frame> | <what is wrong> | <a concrete fix> | <execution|method|direction>
TARGET: <CLOSER|NOT CLOSER|SAME> | <the one respect it most falls short of ref/owner-target.png>
SINGLE-CHANGE: <the one change that would most raise the score>

Read Screenshots

Paste Discord image links (in Discord: open the image, then Copy Link / Copy Media Link) or choose the screenshots, up to 4 at a time. Claude reads each offer into a draft; load one into the form, check it, and SAVE. Discord links expire after about a day. Each read costs roughly a cent or two.

Paste & Classify

Paste the same Discord/email text you'd drop into the app's Drop Inbox. This runs it through the same classifier and lists what it finds - review each draft and click "Load into form" to prefill the entry below, then check the platform name and save.
